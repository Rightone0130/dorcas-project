About Our Department
The Multimedia Technology Department at The Federal Polytechnic, Ilaro is one of the institution's newest and most forward-thinking academic units. Officially established on December 22, 2023, the department came into existence following the approval of the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE) after a successful resource inspection of nineteen programmes. It commenced with a single stream of 60 pioneering students, marking the beginning of a new era in digital innovation within the institution.

Originally extracted from the Mass Communication Department, the Multimedia Technology Department was birthed out of a need to meet the growing demand for skilled professionals in digital media and creative content development. The curriculum is carefully designed to blend creativity with technology, focusing on the integration of text, images, audio, video, and interactive features to produce compelling and dynamic digital content.

Students in the department are exposed to a variety of modern media tools and production techniques. The programme covers a wide array of fields, including:

Multimedia Design
Digital Arts
Animation and Motion Graphics
Audiovisual Production
Interactive Media Development
Web Content Creation
User Interface (UI) and User Experience (UX) Design

With a strong foundation in both the technical and artistic aspects of digital media, students are trained to become versatile professionals who can thrive in industries such as broadcasting, advertising, film production, gaming, e-learning, digital marketing, and more.

The department also places emphasis on practical learning, encouraging hands-on projects, collaborative work, and real-world media production tasks. Students are equipped with relevant skills to adapt to the rapidly evolving digital world and contribute meaningfully to Nigeria’s creative and communication sectors.

As a hub for innovation and digital storytelling, the Multimedia Technology Department continues to evolve, aiming to become a centre of excellence in multimedia education not just in Nigeria, but across West Africa.
